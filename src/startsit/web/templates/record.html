How it's scored

Actual points come from the nflverse weekly stats in the question's scoring format. A player who didn't play scores 0. A tie counts for neither side.

AI: the pick shown on this site. Expert consensus: the FantasyPros consensus on the day asked (the better rank at a position, else the higher projection). Baseline: the code's pick from the consensus projection, adjusted for injuries, byes and your scoring format. The three are compared only on matchups the AI answered. Reddit crowd: start/sit votes from r/fantasyfootball's "Who Do I Start" threads that week, one per comment; it has a pick only when one player leads by {{ crowd_min_lead }}+ net votes (start minus sit), so it's scored on fewer matchups.

The ± is a 95% margin of error. The benchmark matchups are deliberately close calls (players the experts rank side by side), so all three methods are expected to land near 50%. Even a few points above that is a real edge.
