How it works

Code gathers the evidence for each player: the FantasyPros expert consensus, recent usage (snaps, targets, expected points), betting lines, the injury report, this week's news and votes from Reddit's "Who Do I Start" threads. Its starting point is the expert-consensus pick: the higher projection in your scoring format. The AI then makes the call from that evidence alone, citing each fact it used, and has to explain itself when it goes against the experts. Code checks every name, number and citation before you see it. If the AI is busy or out of free answers for the day, you get the expert-consensus pick.

Every pick is scored against what actually happened, next to the experts and the Reddit crowd: see the track record.
